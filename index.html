Lease lengths were read from each individual lot listing and are as published by the auctioneer — always verify against the legal pack before bidding. Station distances come from the Transport for London StopPoint API and are straight-line; walk times apply a 1.25× street-detour factor at 80 m/min, so treat them as indicative rather than door-to-door. The Tube lines overlay is drawn from an open Underground and Docklands Light Railway network dataset in Transport for London’s own line colours. It predates the Elizabeth line and the Northern line’s Battersea extension, so treat it as an orientation layer — each lot’s own station detail, including Elizabeth line access, comes from the live API and is authoritative. Pins are plotted to postcode centroid, so they show the street, not the exact door. Guide prices are not reserves and lots frequently sell above them; buyer’s fees and a contract documentation charge apply on top. Marriage value has not been abolished — the relevant sections of the Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 are not in force, so it remains payable in full on any lease with 80 years or less remaining. On the ranking. The model prices the statutory lease extension under the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 — capitalised ground rent, plus the loss of reversion deferred at the Cadogan v Sportelli rate for flats, plus the freeholder’s half of marriage value below 80 years — and sets the total against what the flat is worth once held. Freehold values start from the HM Land Registry UK House Price Index average flat price for the lot’s borough (August 2026), adjusted for condition and size; that is a borough average, not a valuation of this flat, so override it per lot where you know better. Relativity uses our own curve calibrated to figures cited in Upper Tribunal decisions, not a reproduction of any published graph; tribunals work from the Gerald Eve 2016 and Savills 2016 unenfranchiseable graphs. Ground rents are assumed because catalogues rarely publish them. The stamp duty input is a placeholder, not a calculated rate — set it from your own circumstances. This is a screening tool to decide what deserves a surveyor and a solicitor. It is not a valuation, and it is not investment advice.
